How to play
  1. Everyone plays everyone else once, in a random order. Each bout is one question between two players.
  2. Each player has a side of the screen. When PECK! appears, tap your side (or press Q on the left, P on the right) to peck in, then choose the answer (tap it, or press 1 to 4).
  3. Correct: 2 points and the bout. Wrong: you're locked out, and your opponent can still peck.
  4. Both wrong, or the clock runs out: 0–0, and nobody scores.
  5. Level on points at the end? The bout between those players decides it, then the fastest average correct peck.
