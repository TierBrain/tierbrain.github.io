Bible Quiz Questions and Answers

These Bible quiz questions run from the widely known stories through to detail that rewards proper familiarity with the text. They cover the Old Testament narrative, the life and teaching of Jesus, and the letters and history of the early church. Useful for church groups, youth work and Sunday school as well as general quizzing.

Easy questions 32

  1. Who built an ark to survive a great flood? Noah
  2. In the Bible, who were the first man and woman? Adam and Eve
  3. Which young shepherd defeated the giant Goliath? David
  4. Who was swallowed by a great fish (often called a whale)? Jonah
  5. God created the world in how many days, resting on the seventh? Six
  6. Who led the Israelites out of slavery in Egypt? Moses
  7. What did God give Moses on Mount Sinai? The Ten Commandments
  8. Who was born in a manger in Bethlehem? Jesus
  9. How many disciples (apostles) did Jesus choose? Twelve
  10. Who baptised Jesus in the River Jordan? John the Baptist
  11. What is the first book of the Bible? Genesis
  12. Which garden did Adam and Eve live in? The Garden of Eden
  13. In Eden, who tempted Eve to eat the forbidden fruit? The serpent
  14. Jesus turned water into what at a wedding in Cana? Wine
  15. Who betrayed Jesus for thirty pieces of silver? Judas Iscariot
  16. What is the last book of the Bible? Revelation
  17. Which sea did Moses part for the Israelites to cross? The Red Sea
  18. Who was thrown into a den of lions but was left unharmed? Daniel
  19. What sign did God give Noah after the flood as a promise? A rainbow
  20. Jesus was crucified on a…? Cross
  21. On which day do Christians believe Jesus rose from the dead? The third day
  22. Which strong man lost his strength when his hair was cut? Samson
  23. Who received a coat of many colours from his father? Joseph
  24. In which town was Jesus born? Bethlehem
  25. What did the Israelites worship while Moses was on the mountain? A golden calf
  26. Which apostle denied Jesus three times before the rooster crowed? Peter
  27. The Bible is divided into the Old Testament and the…? New Testament
  28. How many wise men (Magi) are traditionally said to have visited Jesus? Three
  29. The wise men brought gold, frankincense and…? Myrrh
  30. Who was the mother of Jesus? Mary
  31. What do Christians call Jesus's final meal with his disciples? The Last Supper
  32. Jesus fed a great crowd with five loaves and two…? Fish
