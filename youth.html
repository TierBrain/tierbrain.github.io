Why it works in a church hall

Most things you could put in front of a youth group need an app installed, an account made, or a subscription someone has to justify to the treasurer. By the time thirty young people have downloaded something, you have lost the room.

This runs in a web browser. One person opens a game on a laptop or smart TV; everyone else scans a QR code and they are in. It takes under a minute, and it works on the old Android someone borrowed from their mum.

How a session runs

  1. Open a game on the laptop or TV at the front. A five-letter code appears.
  2. Everyone scans the QR code on the screen, or types the code into the site.
  3. They enter a name, pick a team, and play.
  4. You control the pace from the front. Pause, skip, or stop whenever you need to.
