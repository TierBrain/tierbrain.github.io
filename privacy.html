Privacy

You don’t need to register or choose a password to play. Online games use a guest session and store game information so players can play together. This page explains those details, optional emails and analytics.

Last updated 24 October 2026

Who we are

TierBrain runs tierbrain.co. You can reach us at hello@tierbrain.co.

What we collect, and why

  • Page views and performance. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics for page-view and performance statistics. It doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Saved on your device. Your theme, local best scores, sound settings and daily-game progress are kept in your browser’s storage. Some games also save a nickname, room code or current host-game state there to help you resume. Clearing browser data removes these local copies. Nicknames and game information are also sent to the database when you use an online room or save a shared result, as described below.
  • Guest sessions. Our database service, Supabase, creates a guest session for online games. Session identifiers are saved in your browser so the game can recognise the same player or host after a reload. Supabase stores the corresponding guest identity. Clearing browser data can prevent you from resuming that session; it does not automatically delete information already stored on the server.
  • Multiplayer games. Your room code, nickname, team where applicable, game actions, answers, buzzes, progress and results are sent to Supabase to run the game. Players see the in-game details shared by that game, such as nicknames and results. The host receives the information needed to run and score the game.
  • Group leaderboards. If you choose to save a shared result, your nickname, score, level and time left are stored under the group code. These are public shared results. A group code organises a board; it is not a password. Please use a nickname you are happy to share.
  • Wall of Fame. Where a game shows scores on the Wall of Fame, the nickname and score shown are public. Please don’t use your full name. You can ask us to remove an entry at any time.
  • Emails. If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply and, if needed, to fix the problem.
  • Early access. If you submit the optional TierBrain Host form, we store your email address and the intended use you select to manage the early-access list and send its update. You can ask to leave the list by emailing hello@tierbrain.co.

What we don’t collect

We don’t ask you to register an account or provide a password, email address, age or location to play. An early-access email is optional and separate from playing. We don’t sell your data or use it for advertising.

Other services

  • Hosting. Our hosting provider sees standard technical details, such as your IP address, when your browser loads a page.
  • Database and guest sessions. Multiplayer rooms, guest identities, shared leaderboards and the early-access list run on Supabase. It stores the information described above on our behalf.
  • QR codes. When a host’s screen shows a QR code to join, the code image is drawn by a third-party service (QR Server), which receives the join link, including the room code, to do so.
  • Google Fonts. Pages load their fonts from Google, so your browser asks Google’s servers for them and Google receives your IP address.
  • Sharing. The Share button on Today’s question opens your device’s share sheet, or X (Twitter) if your device can’t share. Whatever you share is then governed by that service.
  • Buy Me a Coffee. If you follow the coffee link, you leave TierBrain and their privacy policy applies.

Cookies

TierBrain doesn’t use cookies for tracking or advertising. Online games use browser storage for their guest sessions and game settings. Services you choose to visit from here, such as the coffee link, may set their own cookies.

Children

TierBrain is for everyone, including families. We don’t require a real name, email address or age to play. Parents and carers: please help younger players choose a nickname that isn’t their real name, and keep contact details or other personal information out of game fields.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing hello@tierbrain.co. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled something, you can complain to your local data protection authority. In the UK, that’s the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

How long we keep things

Anything saved in your browser stays until you clear it. Access to a live room is time-limited, but room expiry does not itself mean that stored game records are immediately deleted. Shared results and game records may remain after a game ends. Early-access details are kept for managing the list and sending its update; emails are kept for dealing with the message. To request removal of information stored by TierBrain, email hello@tierbrain.co.

Changes

If we change how TierBrain handles information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
