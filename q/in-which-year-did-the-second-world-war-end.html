In which year did the Second World War end?

Answer
1945

War in Europe ended on 8 May 1945 with Germany's surrender, marked as VE Day. The wider war ended on 2 September 1945 when Japan formally surrendered, following the atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki.
