The Bible game show

Chapter & Verse

Two teams, four rounds and an animated host who runs the whole show. Put it on a TV or laptop; everyone joins on their phone with a code, and the screen keeps score.

  • Two teams of any size
  • Big screen and phones
  • 426 questions

Hold The Throne: Bible

Two players duel on each question; the winner keeps the throne and faces the next challenger from the queue. Build a streak, survive to the Grand Finale, and earn a place among the Legends.

  • Three or more players
  • Big screen and phones
  • 407 questions

Five Bible stories, one big adventure

Full Circuit: Bible Edition, together

One person steps into each story (Samuel’s call, Noah’s flood, Abraham’s stars, Peter in prison and Jonah in the deep) and plays it on their own phone, while everyone else watches the big screen and shouts help. Then the whole group marches round the walls of Jericho.

  • Three to eight players
  • Big screen and phones

Play Full Circuit together

The buzzer race

First Finger: Bible

Everyone sees the same question at once, and the first correct answer takes the point. A wrong buzz locks you out of that question, so be quick but sure.

  • Two or more players
  • Each player on their own phone

Play First Finger

Pull a book, but don’t let it fall

Spines: Bible Edition

A teetering pile of books, each spine a part of the Bible’s story: kings and prophets, the life of Jesus, women of the Bible, parables and miracles. Pull a book, answer its question and set it on top. Lower books score more, but every pull shakes the pile, and whoever topples it is out.

  • Two to eight players, or one alone
  • Big screen and phones

A letter has come through the storm, and the sea has smudged its words. Spin the ship’s helm, answer Bible questions to wipe letters clean, and read the message before the ship runs aground on Malta, where, as in Acts, everyone reaches the shore safely.

  • One player
  • Share a leaderboard with a group code

Running a game night

Four steps, and the screen does the rest.

  1. Open the game on a screen everyone can see. A TV, projector or laptop all work.
  2. Everyone joins on their phone. Scan the code on the screen, or type it in, then enter a first name or nickname.
  3. Start the game. The screen runs the questions and keeps the score; you just enjoy the noise.
  4. Try it once beforehand. Test on the same Wi-Fi you’ll use on the night, so you know how it looks in your room.
