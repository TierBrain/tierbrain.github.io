Five Bible stories, one big adventure

Full Circuit: Bible Edition Team

One person steps into each story (Samuel’s call, Noah’s flood, Abraham’s stars, Peter in prison and Jonah in the deep) and plays it on their own phone, while everyone else watches the big screen and shouts help. Then the whole group marches round the walls of Jericho together.

  • Big screen and phones
  • Three to eight players
  • Best when the room gets loud

The buzzer race

First Finger: Bible

Everyone sees the same question at once, and the first correct answer takes the point. A wrong buzz locks you out of that question, so be quick but sure.

  • Each player on their own phone
  • Two or more players
Play First Finger

Pull a book, but don’t let it fall

Spines: Bible Edition

A teetering pile of books, each spine a part of the Bible’s story: kings and prophets, the life of Jesus, women of the Bible, parables and miracles. Pull a book, answer its question and set it on top. Lower books score more, but every pull shakes the pile, and whoever topples it is out.

  • Big screen and phones
  • Two to eight players
  • Or play alone

Running a game night

Four steps, and the screen does the rest.

  1. Open the game on a screen everyone can see.A TV, projector or laptop all work.
  2. Everyone joins on their phone.Scan the code on the screen, or type it in, then enter a first name or nickname.
  3. Start the game.The screen runs the questions and keeps the score; you just enjoy the noise.
  4. Try it once beforehand.Test on the same Wi-Fi you’ll use on the night, so you know how it looks in your room.
