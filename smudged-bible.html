Smudged

A bottle has washed ashore, and the sea has smudged its message. Spin the helm to choose your waters, answer to earn letters, and read the message before your ship sinks.

Calm waters give one letter, the open sea two, and a storm three, the only way to earn a vowel. Every letter still smudged when you solve is worth ten points. A wrong answer springs a leak; three, and she sinks.
