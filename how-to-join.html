Chapter & Verse · a Bible game show
How to join
It takes about 20 seconds. Just grab your phone.
1

Open your phone's camera

Point it at the QR code on the big screen. No app needed.

2

Tap the link that pops up

Or type tierbrain.github.io/play.html into your browser.

3

Enter the code & your name

The 5-letter code is on the big screen. Your name shows on the scoreboard.

4

Tap your team, then play

Choose Youth or Adults. Read each question on the big screen and answer on your phone.
